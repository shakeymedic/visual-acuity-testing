Visual Acuity

ED Assessment Guide

UK Standards

6 Metres

Standard Snellen distance. (Or 3m with mirror)

Glasses ON

Wear distance correction. Remove reading glasses.

One Eye

Test separately. Occlude fully (no peeking).

1 The Procedure

R

Right Eye first. Occlude left. Read from top down.

Push limits. Record smallest line with < 2 errors.

L

Repeat for Left Eye.

2 The Pinhole Test

WHEN TO USE

If Visual Acuity is 6/12 or worse

IMPROVES

Refractive Error

Likely needs new glasses. Usually not an emergency.

NO CHANGE

Pathology

Cataract, retinal issue, or macula. Needs exam.
